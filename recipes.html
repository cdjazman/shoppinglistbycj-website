How it works

Three taps, done.

1

Pick a recipe

Browse the ingredient lists below — no account, no sign-up.

2

Tap “Add to Shopping List”

We hand the ingredients to the app as a link — nothing is stored on our servers, because we don't have any.

3

Shop it

Every ingredient lands on your current list, matched against your existing catalogue where possible.

Recipes

Weeknight favourites

A starter set of everyday recipes, built around ingredients you'll find at Aldi, Coles or Woolworths.

Butter Chicken

Serves 4 · ~40 min

A rich, mildly spiced tomato and cream curry — a weeknight favourite that's easier to make than it looks.

  • 700g chicken thigh fillets, diced
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tub Greek yoghurt
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 bulb garlic
  • 1 knob ginger
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 tub thickened cream
  • 1 block butter
  • 1kg basmati rice
  • 1 bunch coriander

Spaghetti Bolognese

Serves 4 · ~45 min

A slow-simmered beef and tomato sauce over spaghetti — the kind of meal that's better the longer it cooks.

  • 500g spaghetti
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 bulb garlic
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 sticks celery, diced
  • 2 cans diced tomatoes
  • 1L beef stock
  • 1 block parmesan, grated
  • Olive oil

Vegetable Fried Rice

Serves 3 · ~25 min

A fast, freezer-friendly fried rice that's a good way to use up whatever's left in the fridge.

  • 1kg jasmine rice
  • 1 dozen eggs
  • 1 bag frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 bunch spring onion
  • Soy sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • 1 bulb garlic
  • 1 knob ginger

Classic Beef Tacos

Serves 4 · ~25 min

Build-your-own tacos — always a safe bet for a night when everyone wants something different.

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1 box taco shells
  • 1 block cheddar cheese, grated
  • 1 lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 tub sour cream
  • 1 brown onion, diced

Honey Soy Salmon with Greens

Serves 2 · ~20 min

A quick, sticky pan-seared salmon with a fast side of greens — on the table in well under half an hour.

  • 2 salmon fillets
  • Honey
  • Soy sauce
  • 1 bulb garlic
  • 1 bunch broccolini
  • 1 handful green beans
  • Sesame oil
  • 1 bunch spring onion

Loaded Nachos

Serves 4, sharing · ~30 min

A tray of nachos built for the middle of the table — good for a casual night in with friends.

  • 1 bag corn chips
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1 can refried beans
  • 1 block cheddar cheese, grated
  • 1 jar jalapeños
  • 1 tub sour cream
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 bunch spring onion
